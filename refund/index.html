Refunds · Updated Jan 2026

Fair refunds, in writing.

Changed your mind? The earlier you tell us, the more you get back.

First 48 hours During design During build After launch Care plans How to request
1

First 48 hours · 100% back

Changed your mind

Cancel within 48 hours of paying the advance, before design starts, and the full advance returns to your source account within 7 working days. No questions, no fees.

2

During design · 50% back

Design in progress

Once wireframes or UI work has started, half the advance covers design time. The other half is refunded, and all drafts so far are handed over to you.

3

During build · Pause, not refund

Build in progress

After design approval the advance is committed to build time, so it is non-refundable. You can pause up to 90 days and resume free, or take the complete code-so-far and walk away.

4

After launch · Fix, not refund

Already live

Launched sites are covered by 30-day free support instead of refunds. Anything broken against the agreed scope gets fixed free. New asks are quoted separately.

5

Care plans · Pro-rata

Monthly plans

Cancel any time. The current month is not refunded, future months are never charged, and backups plus credentials are handed over within 3 days.

6

How to request

One email does it

Mail hello@melbourn.dev with your project name and UPI / account details. Approved refunds land within 7 working days. Disputes first go to a goodwill call before anything formal.
