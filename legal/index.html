01 · Privacy policy

Your data stays yours.

What we collect

Contact details you share (name, email, phone), project content (logos, copy, images) and basic analytics (pages visited, device type). We never ask for passwords or OTPs.

How we use it

Only to reply to enquiries, deliver projects, send invoices and improve the site. No spam, no cold outreach to your customers, ever.

Cookies & analytics

We use privacy-friendly analytics (GA4 with IP anonymization) to see which pages help visitors. No ad trackers, no cross-site profiling.

Sharing & retention

We do not sell data. Processors who touch it: our hosting provider, payment gateway and email tools, each bound by their own policies. Project files are kept 12 months after handover for support, then deleted on request.

Your rights

Ask for a copy, correction or deletion any time at hello@melbourn.dev. We respond within 7 working days.

02 · Terms & conditions

Fair terms, both sides.

Scope & quotes

Every project starts with a written quote listing deliverables, timeline and price. Anything outside that list is scoped and priced separately before work begins.

Payments

50% advance to book, 50% on launch. UPI, cards and bank transfer accepted. Work pauses if a due payment is delayed over 7 days; launch dates shift by the same delay.

Revisions & delays

Two revision rounds per stage are included. Extra rounds are billed at the hourly rate in your quote. If client feedback stalls over 14 days, the project moves to maintenance billing until it resumes.

Ownership & IP

On final payment, the website design and code become yours, including a full handover. We retain the right to show the work in our portfolio unless an NDA says otherwise. Stock assets follow their own licenses.

Liability & law

Liability is capped at the project fee paid. We are not liable for third-party outages (hosting, payment gateways) or content you supply. These terms are governed by the laws of India, with jurisdiction in Surat, Gujarat.
